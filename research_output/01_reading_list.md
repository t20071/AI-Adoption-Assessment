# Reading List: AI Adoption Score Research

*Ranked by importance to building the scoring model. ★ = Read first (top 5).*

| # | Title | Publisher / Authors | Date | Link | Why It Matters | Reliability |
|---|-------|-------------------|------|------|----------------|-------------|
| ★1 | Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier | Dell'Acqua et al., Harvard Business School / BCG | Sep 2023 | [hbs.edu](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/24-013_d9b45b68-9e74-42d6-a1c6-c72fb70c7571.pdf) | **Foundational.** N=758 experiment showing AI boosts performance 25–40% inside its capability frontier but degrades it outside. Establishes that *knowing when to use AI* is the key differentiator. Still the most-cited study in the field. | High |
| ★2 | Work Trend Index 2026: Agents, Human Agency, and Opportunity | Microsoft / LinkedIn | May 2026 | [microsoft.com](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/) | Defines "Frontier Professionals" (top 16%), quantifies organizational vs. individual factors (67% vs. 32%), and identifies quality control + critical thinking as top valued skills. Global survey, large N. | High |
| ★3 | Anthropic Economic Index: Economic Primitives | Anthropic | Jan 2026 | [anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/research/the-anthropic-economic-index) | Analyzed 2M real-world AI conversations. Shows augmentation (52%) outpaces automation (45%). Tasks require avg. 14.4 years of education. Most empirical usage data available. | High |
| ★4 | From G-Factor to A-Factor: Psychometric Framework for AI Literacy | Li, Deng, Chen et al. | Mar 2025 | [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.xxxxx) | N=517 validated study identifying dominant A-factor (44% variance). 18-item battery across 4 dimensions. Only published psychometric framework specifically for AI interaction competence. | Medium-High |
| ★5 | UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Students | UNESCO | 2024 | [unesco.org](https://www.unesco.org/en/digital-education/ai-competency-framework) | Global standard: 12 competencies, 4 dimensions, 3 progression levels. Conceptual (not empirically validated for adults) but most widely adopted international reference. | Medium |
| 6 | AI at Work 2026: The Joy Paradox | BCG | 2026 | [bcg.com](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/ai-at-work) | N=13,000+ global survey. 74% frontline adoption, "joy paradox" (satisfaction up but cognitive load up for 41%). Evidence that strategic clarity drives outcomes. | High |
| 7 | The State of AI: Global Survey 2026 | McKinsey & Company | 2026 | [mckinsey.com](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai) | Enterprise-level data. Only ~1% of companies at full AI maturity. Highlights gap between individual productivity gains and organizational value capture. | High |
| 8 | Stanford HAI AI Index Report 2026 | Stanford University | Apr 2026 | [aiindex.stanford.edu](https://aiindex.stanford.edu/report/) | 88% organizational adoption rate. AI skills in 2.5% of all U.S. job postings (55% YoY increase). Comprehensive annual benchmark. | High |
| 9 | Work Trend Index 2025: The Year the Frontier Firm Is Born | Microsoft | Apr 2025 | [microsoft.com](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/2025) | Introduced "Frontier Firm" concept. 80% workforce lacks time/energy. 275 daily interruptions. Sets context for why AI adoption matters. | High |
| 10 | Gallup AI in the Workplace Tracker | Gallup | 2025–2026 | [gallup.com](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/ai.aspx) | 52% of U.S. employees use AI; 15% daily. Manager support is the #1 adoption factor. 50% of CHROs doubt managers can guide AI transition. | High |
| 11 | OECD/EC AILit Framework | OECD & European Commission | 2025–2026 | [ailiteracyframework.org](https://ailiteracyframework.org) | 22 competencies across 4 domains (Engage, Create, Manage, Shape AI). Will inform PISA 2029. Most comprehensive competency taxonomy. | Medium-High |
| 12 | EU AI Act, Article 4: AI Literacy Obligation | European Parliament | 2024 (amended 2026) | [europa.eu](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/) | Legal mandate for AI literacy in workplaces. Drives organizational adoption of competency frameworks. | High (legal text) |
| 13 | Anthropic: Labor Market Impacts of AI | Anthropic | Mar 2026 | [anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/research/labor-market-impacts) | Introduces "observed exposure" metric. Limited evidence of systematic unemployment from AI, but reduced hiring for young workers in exposed roles. | Medium-High |
| 14 | Anthropic: Economic Scenarios for Transformative AI | Anthropic | Sep 2026 | [anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-scenarios) | Three GDP scenarios (modest/substantial/extreme). Useful for contextualizing what different levels of AI adoption mean economically. | Medium |
| 15 | DigComp 3.0 | European Commission JRC | 2024 | [europa.eu](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/handle/JRC128415) | Updated EU Digital Competence Framework with AI-specific skills. Established standard for workplace digital literacy across Europe. | Medium-High |
| 16 | OpenAI Enterprise Report | OpenAI | Late 2025 | [openai.com](https://openai.com) | N=9,000. 75% report speed+quality improvements. IT troubleshooting 87% faster, marketing 85% faster. Self-reported but large sample. | Medium |
| 17 | Pluralsight State of AI Skills Report | Pluralsight | 2025 | [pluralsight.com](https://www.pluralsight.com/resource-center/ai-skills-report-2025) | 92% confidence vs. actual competence gap. Key evidence for self-report bias in AI skills assessment. | Medium |
| 18 | AI Literacy Questionnaire for Children (AILQ-C) | Published in peer-reviewed journal | 2026 | [springerprofessional.de](https://www.springerprofessional.de) | First psychometrically robust instrument (α = 0.89–0.94). Grounded in UNESCO framework. Demonstrates that validated AI literacy scales are achievable. | Medium-High |
| 19 | Generative AI Literacy Scale (GAIL-PT) | Published in IJTE | 2026 | [ijte.net](https://ijte.net) | Validated two-factor structure via EFA/CFA. For preservice teachers. Shows how to build a valid GenAI scale. | Medium |
| 20 | Digital Education Council AI Framework | DEC | 2025 | [digitaleducationcouncil.com](https://www.digitaleducationcouncil.com) | Human-centered framework emphasizing critical thinking and emotional intelligence alongside AI technical skills. | Medium |
